title: LeashLock privacy policy
---

_Effective 20 November 2026_

LeashLock is an Android app. It locks your phone when the Bluetooth devices you chose (a watch, earbuds,
headphones and so on) disconnect. This policy explains what the app does with data. In short, it collects nothing
and sends nothing anywhere.

## What the app collects

Nothing. LeashLock has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It doesn't request the Internet
permission, so it can't send data off your phone.

## Data that stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps a small amount of data in its own private storage on your device:

- **Your chosen devices.** The Bluetooth name and address of each device you added. Android's Companion Device
  Manager holds this list.
- **Settings.** Your choices, such as the lock method and the grace period.
- **Event log.** The last 100 decisions, for example "watch disconnected, phone locked", with the time and the
  device's Bluetooth address. You can clear the log in the app.

Android may include this data in your own device backup if backup is turned on in your phone's settings. That backup
belongs to your Google account. The developer never receives a copy.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this data.

## Permissions and why they're needed

- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect):** to see when your chosen devices connect and disconnect.
- **Companion device access:** so Android can tell the app when a chosen device comes near or goes away, and keep
  the app running in the background.
- **Notifications:** for the ongoing "protecting" notification, and to warn you if the phone couldn't be locked.
- **Device admin (optional):** used only to lock the screen. The app requests no other admin power. It can't wipe,
  reset or change your password.
- **Accessibility service (optional):** used only to lock the screen, the same as pressing the power button. It
  reads no screen content, notifications, messages or typing, and it receives no accessibility events.

## Sharing

The app shares no data with anyone, because it has none to share.

## Children

The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.
